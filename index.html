ABOUT THE CHANNEL

Hi, I'm Abhiyukth!

Welcome to Abhiyukth Vlogs! Based in Kerala, I create authentic Malayalam video content sharing the thrill of PS5 gaming, live multiplayer sessions, and candid travel journeys.

From long train rides across India (Mangala Lakshadweep Express, Jammu & Kashmir journeys, Rajasthan) to local temple festivals and Kozhikode walkathons, this channel is about celebrating real stories, good games, and unforgettable adventures.
